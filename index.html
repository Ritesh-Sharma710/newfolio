About

I’m a Computer Science Undergraduate passionate about systems engineering, Linux, cloud infrastructure, and backend development. I enjoy building software that is reliable, scalable, and efficient—from Unix shells and distributed systems to networking tools and cloud-native applications. I believe the best engineering happens by understanding how things work beneath the surface and solving problems from first principles. Constantly learning, building, and experimenting, I’m driven to create technology that delivers measurable impact while continuously sharpening my technical depth through hands-on projects, open-source learning, and real-world challenges.
